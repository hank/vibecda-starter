Click the game, then press Enter. Type the letter or word above a zombie to shoot it. In the higher ranks, tougher monsters carry a phrase: each coloured word breaks one piece of armour, with a space between words. Typing without a mistake earns better weapons at streaks of 10, 25 and 50; one wrong key drops you back to oak arrows. When a boss monster arrives, move to the arrow keys and hit the arrows on the beat. Backspace drops your target; Esc pauses, and clicking away pauses too. This game needs a physical keyboard.

Written in C on SDL3 and compiled to WebAssembly. Art and music are generated in code.
