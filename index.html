Click the game, pick a game with ← → (typing, math, or the Idaho driver's test), then press Enter. In math, type the answer to the sum a monster carries; in the driver's test, press the letter of the right answer. Type the letter or word above a zombie to shoot it. In the higher ranks, tougher monsters carry a phrase: each coloured word breaks one piece of armour, with a space between words. Monsters drop coins. After each rank you visit the shop, where coins buy better weapons (kept for good), wall potions, and frost charms. A run that makes the high-score board asks for your name; F3 on the title screen shows the board. If the arrows feel early or late, press F2 on the title screen: first tap along to the click by ear, then tap along to the arrows by eye. F6 saves the game and F7 loads it. Backspace drops your target; Esc pauses, and clicking away pauses too. This game needs a physical keyboard.

Driver's test questions are practice questions written from the Idaho Driver's Handbook (July 2026); they are not the official test. Written in C on SDL3 and compiled to WebAssembly. Art and music are generated in code.
